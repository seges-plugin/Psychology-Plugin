# Psychology

Guided workflows help you reflect on context you choose and turn useful ideas into a practical next step. This package uses the identifier `psychology-workflows` and connects the **Psychology** MCP server at `https://noesis.seges.ai/mcp`.

The package includes reflection, context, assessment, interpretation and coaching workflows with supporting references. It contains no root agents or local hooks. Use your host's supported installation interface and complete Noesis OAuth in the browser.

Start with your current question or goal. Choose relevant source material and review the examples behind suggestions. You can correct, skip or reject them. Your host can use only the context it actually makes available; an inaccessible conversation archive stays inaccessible.

Reading stored records requires the relevant consent and source choice. Saving eligible information uses the actual destination's permission and exact owner-review flow. Your assistant cannot approve changes for you. Imported context, personal profiles and workspace records keep separate controls. A working draft is not proof of a saved record.

Use [Today](https://noesis.seges.ai/en/today/) for tasks and reminders, or available connected operations with their separate permissions. Check an unclear save result before creating another record.

Psychometric tools support non-clinical reflection, not diagnosis or treatment. Original item instructions, instrument rights and your own confirmed responses matter. Professional listings are unverified leads. This is not an emergency service.

Psychology is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not for children under 13 and is unavailable in the EU, EEA and UK. Follow your AI host's age and parent or legal guardian permission requirements. Use only features permitted by your current account controls.

Individual exercises and instruments may have stricter age or suitability requirements; general access does not make every assessment appropriate for ages 13–17. Relationship reflection is non-explicit and focuses on communication, consent, personal boundaries and respectful relationships.
