# Psychology

Use Psychology to clarify a question, explore a reflection exercise or work with Noesis records you choose. This package connects MCP tools without bundled skills or local hooks.

Connect to `https://noesis.seges.ai/mcp` using your host's browser OAuth flow. Available operations depend on your host and account permissions.

Choose the source before reading stored records. Saving a change requires its applicable authorization and review. Installing a connection does not start background account reads or saving, and it cannot retrieve an unavailable conversation archive.

Psychometric results support non-clinical reflection, not diagnosis or treatment. Answer exercises in your own words; missing item instructions or scales should remain unresolved rather than invented. Professional listings are unverified leads. This is not an emergency service.

For tasks and reminders, use [Today](https://noesis.seges.ai/en/today/) or available connected task operations with their separate permissions. You can continue with a draft when an operation is unavailable. If a save result is unclear, check the original operation before repeating it.

Psychology is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not for children under 13 and is unavailable in the EU, EEA and UK. Follow your AI host's age and parent or legal guardian permission requirements. Use only features permitted by your current account controls.

Individual exercises and instruments may have stricter age or suitability requirements; general access does not make every assessment appropriate for ages 13–17. Relationship reflection is non-explicit and focuses on communication, consent, personal boundaries and respectful relationships.
