# Psychology

**Your plan changed. Keep moving.**

Psychology helps you review a goal, understand what changed and decide what to do next using context you choose. Continue the work in your AI, with Noesis tasks and reminders when available.

[Open Today](https://noesis.seges.ai/en/today/) · [Get support](https://noesis.seges.ai/support/)

## Start with something you need to do

- Compare an original agreement with a new request and draft a reply.
- Review a changed goal alongside your current limits and possible next steps.
- Turn an agreed next step into a Today task, with optional dates or reminders.
- Explore a reflection exercise when it is useful to your question.

For example: “My client asked for extra work, but Friday's deadline stays the same. Help me compare the agreement with this message and prepare a reply.” Share the material you want to use, review the suggestions and choose your next step.

Tell your assistant what you want help with. It can use context available in the current conversation and other sources you deliberately select. It cannot retrieve an unavailable conversation archive.

## Install in your AI

### Add to Claude.ai

Open [Claude Plugins](https://claude.ai/new#settings/customize-plugins), add the repository `https://github.com/seges-plugin/Psychology-Plugin` and install **Psychology**. Connect to Noesis through the browser sign-in when prompted.

### Use Psychology in ChatGPT

<a id="use-Psychology-plugin-in-chatgpt"></a>

When custom MCP connections are available in your account, connect **Psychology** at `https://noesis.seges.ai/mcp` and authorize the connection in Noesis.

Available features depend on the host and your connected account.

## Your context and choices

You choose the source material and can correct, skip or reject a suggestion. Reading stored records requires the relevant permission and your choice of source. Saving eligible changes uses the applicable review and authorization flow. Signing in or installing the package does not start background reads or saving.

You can continue the conversation while a save waits for your review. Imported context, personal profile information and workspace records have separate controls.

## Keep the same goal in view

Ask for a comparison, a draft or a task in the same conversation. You do not need to fill out a complete profile first. [Today](https://noesis.seges.ai/en/today/) is your Noesis task and reminder view. Connected task and record features require the relevant account permissions. If a feature is unavailable in your AI, keep the draft or continue on the website.

## Age and availability

Psychology is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not for children under 13 and is unavailable in the EU, EEA and UK. Follow your AI host's age and parent or legal guardian permission requirements. Use only features permitted by your current account controls.

Individual exercises and instruments may have stricter age or suitability requirements; general access does not make every assessment appropriate for ages 13–17. Relationship reflection is non-explicit and focuses on communication, consent, personal boundaries and respectful relationships.

## Support and wellbeing

Psychology supports personal reflection and next-step planning. It does not provide diagnosis or treatment and is not an emergency service. Instrument results need their original context and applicable use conditions; professional listings are unverified leads rather than clinical endorsements.
