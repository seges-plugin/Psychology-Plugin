# Psychology

**Think it through. Then take the next step.**

Psychology helps you reflect on experiences you choose, understand useful patterns and turn an idea into a practical next step.

[Connect your AI](docs/setup-and-contract.md) · [Open Today](https://noesis.seges.ai/en/today/) · [Get support](https://noesis.seges.ai/support/)

## What you can do

- Clarify a question or goal using relevant context you choose.
- Explore self-reflection exercises and understand the returned results.
- Review suggestions, correct them and decide what to keep.
- Organize a next step in Today, with dates or reminders when useful.

Tell your assistant what you want help with. It can use context available in the current conversation and other sources you deliberately select. It cannot retrieve an unavailable conversation archive.

## Install in your AI

### Add to Claude.ai

Open [Claude Plugins](https://claude.ai/new#settings/customize-plugins), add the repository `https://github.com/seges-plugin/Psychology-Plugin` and install **Psychology**. Connect to Noesis through the browser sign-in when prompted.

### Use Psychology in ChatGPT

<a id="use-Psychology-plugin-in-chatgpt"></a>

When custom MCP connections are available in your account, connect **Psychology** at `https://noesis.seges.ai/mcp` and authorize the connection in Noesis.

See [installation and account access](docs/setup-and-contract.md) for other hosts. Available features depend on the host and your connected account.

## Your context and choices

You choose the source material and can correct, skip or reject a suggestion. Reading stored records requires the relevant permission and your choice of source. Saving eligible changes uses the applicable review and authorization flow. Signing in or installing the package does not start background reads or saving.

You can continue the conversation while a save waits for your review. Imported context, personal profile information and workspace records have separate controls. See [context and memory](docs/activation-and-memory.md).

## Age and availability

Psychology is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not for children under 13 and is unavailable in the EU, EEA and UK. Follow your AI host's age and parent or legal guardian permission requirements. Use only features permitted by your current account controls.

Individual exercises and instruments may have stricter age or suitability requirements; general access does not make every assessment appropriate for ages 13–17. Relationship reflection is non-explicit and focuses on communication, consent, personal boundaries and respectful relationships.

## Support and wellbeing

Psychology is a noncommercial reflection tool. It does not provide diagnosis or treatment and is not an emergency service. Instrument results need their original context; professional listings are unverified leads rather than clinical endorsements.

Manage permissions, request a data copy or request account deletion in your [Noesis account](https://noesis.seges.ai/en/account/).
