# Safety Guidance for Noesis Interactions

This reference applies when a user presents a current safety concern during coaching, an assessment, or an interpretation. `skills/crisis-support/SKILL.md` is the canonical detailed response sequence; the host-selected `agents/safety-monitor.md` is a compact execution aid. Neither is an executable monitor, an automatic classifier, or a promise that an outside person can be contacted.

## Priority order

1. Pause the normal task. Do not use scores, account memory, journal/profile tools, context distillation, or provider lookup to decide whether the concern is real.
2. Acknowledge what the person has shared and ask only the direct, minimal questions needed to understand immediate safety.
3. If there may be immediate danger, encourage location-appropriate emergency or crisis help now and invite the person to contact an available trusted person or move to a safer place. Do not claim that the plugin, a host, or a hidden agent contacted anyone.
4. Offer a short current-session safety plan and practical grounding options only if the person wants them. Avoid interrogation, diagnosis, or turning the disclosure into assessment evidence.
5. Summarize options in the current response when helpful. There is no scheduled follow-up, account flag, background monitoring, or safety event log supplied by this bundle.

## Provider lookup after the acute moment

Only after the person says the immediate situation is stable, and only at their explicit request for ongoing local support, resolve the accepted host's exact tool name and call `noesisfind_counselors(location, focus, max_results)`. A result is a directory listing, not a vetted recommendation or proof of availability, qualification, fit, or emergency capacity. Relay any limits the tool returns. Never wait for this lookup before offering immediate resources.

## Boundaries and continuity

- Do not diagnose, promise safety, promise recovery, or represent a result as professional care.
- Do not write a journal entry, save a profile, or save a result from a safety exchange unless the person later makes a separate, informed request and completes the normal consent flow.
- If the person returns later, work only from what is visible or newly selected through the session bootstrap; do not claim that the plugin followed up or remembered an unselected prior exchange.
- Resume the interrupted task only when the person explicitly chooses it and current safety concerns no longer take priority.
