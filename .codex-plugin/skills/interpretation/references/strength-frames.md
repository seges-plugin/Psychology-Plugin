# Strength Frames, All 30 OCEAN Facets

> This document provides strength-framed descriptions for every OCEAN facet at every score level. Use it to generate positive, neurodivergent-affirming interpretations regardless of where someone scores.

## Format

Each facet includes:
- **Very High (90%+)**, Distinctive strength framing
- **High (70-89%)**, Clear strength framing
- **Average (30-69%)**, Flexible range framing
- **Low (10-29%)**, Selective focus framing
- **Very Low (<10%)**, Unique pattern framing

---

## Openness to Experience

### Imagination (O1), Capacity for inner world-building

**Very High:** You have an extraordinarily rich inner world. Your mind generates possibilities, scenarios, and entire worlds with ease. This makes you a natural creative thinker, storyteller, and visionary. The challenge can be grounding your visions in reality or knowing when to turn the imagination "off." Many ND creators find their imagination is both their greatest gift and what keeps them awake at night.

**High:** You have a well-developed imagination that serves you well in creative problem-solving and envisioning possibilities. You can entertain multiple futures and explore ideas that others might dismiss. You likely enjoy fiction, daydreaming, or creative projects.

**Average:** Your imagination is situationally activated, you can engage it when needed but don't live in it constantly. This gives you a nice balance between creative thinking and present-moment focus.

**Low:** You're someone who prefers concrete reality to hypothetical possibilities. This isn't a lack, it's a strength. You don't get lost in "what-ifs," which means you can take action without overthinking. Your practical orientation frees up mental energy for execution.

**Very Low:** You have a distinctly present-focused, concrete way of engaging with the world. While others get distracted by possibilities, you stay grounded in what is. This makes you remarkably effective at cutting through complexity and focusing on actionable reality.

### Artistic Interests (O2), Appreciation for aesthetic experience

**Very High:** You have a profound sensitivity to beauty, art, and aesthetic experience. Music, visual art, nature, or design likely moves you deeply. This capacity for aesthetic appreciation enriches your life in ways that many people don't experience. You may be drawn to creative expression yourself.

**High:** You have a genuine appreciation for art, beauty, and creative expression. You likely enjoy museums, music, design, or nature. This sensibility adds richness and meaning to your everyday experience.

**Average:** You enjoy aesthetic experiences when you encounter them, but don't actively seek them out. You can appreciate a beautiful sunset or a well-designed space without making aesthetics a central focus.

**Low:** You're more interested in function than form. You appreciate things that work well more than things that look beautiful. This practical orientation means you don't get distracted by aesthetics and can focus on what matters most to you.

**Very Low:** You have a strongly utilitarian approach to the world. You value what works over what looks good. This is a genuine strength, you're immune to superficial appeal and can see through marketing, pretension, and unnecessary complexity.

### Emotionality (O3), Emotional depth and richness

**Very High:** You experience emotions with exceptional depth and complexity. Your emotional life is rich, nuanced, and deeply felt. This gives you profound empathy, emotional insight, and the capacity for deeply meaningful relationships. The intensity can sometimes be overwhelming, but it's also the source of your greatest connections.

**High:** You have good access to your emotions and experience them vividly. You're likely comfortable with emotional expression and can articulate how you feel. This emotional awareness serves your relationships and self-understanding well.

**Average:** You experience emotions in a balanced way, present but not overwhelming. You can access your feelings when needed without being ruled by them.

**Low:** You tend to approach life with emotional steadiness. You don't get swept away by feelings, which gives you clarity during emotional situations. This stability is a real asset in crisis and high-pressure moments.

**Very Low:** You have a remarkable capacity for emotional equilibrium. While others may be tossed by emotional storms, you maintain steady clarity. This doesn't mean you don't care, you may care deeply but process emotions differently, through action or thought rather than feeling.

### Adventurousness (O4), Willingness to try new experiences

**Very High:** You have an insatiable appetite for novelty and new experience. You're likely always exploring, new ideas, places, foods, hobbies. This curiosity keeps you growing and prevents stagnation. The challenge is follow-through; you may have many unfinished projects. But the breadth of your exploration is genuinely impressive.

**High:** You're open to new experiences and enjoy variety in life. You probably like trying new things, visiting new places, or learning about unfamiliar topics. This keeps life interesting and helps you build a broad knowledge base.

**Average:** You enjoy some novelty but also value familiarity. You're open to new experiences in some areas while preferring the known in others. This selective openness serves you well.

**Low:** You know what you like, and you stick with it. This isn't rigidity, it's discernment. You've done the exploring and found what works for you. Your preferences are well-calibrated, and you don't waste energy on things that don't serve you.

**Very Low:** You have exceptionally well-honed preferences and deep expertise in what you love. Rather than spreading yourself thin across many experiences, you've invested deeply in what matters most to you. This depth over breadth is a genuine strength.

### Intellect (O5), Love of ideas and intellectual engagement

**Very High:** You have an intense intellectual appetite. Ideas excite you. Complex problems energize you. You likely love learning, debating, analyzing, and thinking deeply. Your mind is always active, always seeking to understand. This intellectual vitality is one of your defining strengths.

**High:** You enjoy intellectual engagement and thinking deeply about topics that interest you. You probably like learning, reading, or discussing ideas. Your mind is active and engaged.

**Average:** You can engage intellectually when motivated but don't need constant mental stimulation. You're comfortable with both deep thinking and lighter mental activities.

**Low:** You prefer practical application over abstract theorizing. You want to know how something works, not just how it could work. This action-oriented approach means you spend less time in analysis paralysis and more time doing.

**Very Low:** You have a beautifully practical, action-oriented mind. While others get lost in theory, you cut straight to what matters and what works. This directness is refreshing and effective. You're the person who gets things moving while others are still debating.

### Liberalism (O6), Openness to unconventional ideas

**Very High:** You have a strong independent streak and resist conventional thinking. You question norms, challenge assumptions, and think for yourself. This nonconformity can sometimes create friction with traditional structures, but it's also the source of genuine innovation and progress.

**High:** You're open to unconventional ideas and don't accept things just because "that's how it's done." You have your own perspective and aren't afraid to voice it.

**Average:** You're generally open to new ideas while respecting useful traditions. You can evaluate unconventional thinking on its merits without automatically rejecting or accepting it.

**Low:** You value tried-and-true approaches and proven methods. This respect for tradition means you don't chase every new trend. You build on solid foundations, which creates lasting results.

**Very Low:** You have deep respect for established wisdom and proven systems. While others chase novelty, you understand the value of what has worked over time. This conservatism isn't closed-mindedness, it's wisdom about what endures.

## Conscientiousness

### Self-Efficacy (C1), Belief in one's ability to accomplish tasks

**Very High:** You have exceptional confidence in your ability to get things done. When you set your mind to something, you believe you can achieve it, and you usually do. This self-assurance is contagious and empowering. You're the person others look to when something difficult needs to happen.

**High:** You generally believe in your ability to accomplish what you set out to do. You trust your skills and judgment, which helps you take on challenges without excessive self-doubt.

**Average:** Your confidence varies by domain, strong in areas of expertise, more cautious in unfamiliar territory. This calibrated self-efficacy serves you well.

**Low:** You're modest about your abilities and may underestimate what you can achieve. While this humility is genuine, it may sometimes hold you back from attempting things you're actually capable of. Many ND adults develop this pattern from years of being told they "should" be able to do things that don't match their strengths.

**Very Low:** You have significant doubts about your capabilities, which may not match your actual competence. This gap between self-perception and ability is common for ND adults who've spent years in environments that didn't fit. Your actual abilities may far exceed what you believe about yourself.

### Orderliness (C2), Preference for organization and structure

**Very High:** You have a strong need for order, systems, and organization. You likely have well-structured routines, organized spaces, and clear systems. This organizational capacity is a genuine superpower, you can create structure where others see chaos. The challenge is flexibility when plans change unexpectedly.

**High:** You prefer organized environments and structured approaches. You likely keep your space tidy and appreciate having systems in place. This organization helps you function effectively.

**Average:** You can be organized when it's important but don't need everything perfectly structured. You have a functional level of order that serves you without becoming restrictive.

**Low:** You have a more relaxed, flexible approach to organization. You may find that strict systems feel constraining. Your strength is adaptability, you can function well in less structured environments that might overwhelm more order-dependent people.

**Very Low:** You thrive in flexibility and resist rigid structure. While this can look like "disorganization" to others, it's actually a different organizational style, one based on emergence, intuition, and organic flow. Many creative ND adults have this pattern. The challenge is finding systems that support your natural style rather than fighting it.

### Dutifulness (C3), Sense of obligation and responsibility

**Very High:** You have an exceptionally strong sense of duty and responsibility. When you commit to something, you follow through, period. This reliability makes you incredibly trustworthy. Others know they can count on you. The challenge is setting boundaries so you don't take on too much.

**High:** You have a well-developed sense of obligation and generally follow through on commitments. You're reliable and take your responsibilities seriously.

**Average:** You honor your important commitments while allowing yourself flexibility with minor obligations. You have a healthy balance of responsibility and self-care.

**Low:** You're selective about your obligations, you commit to what truly matters to you and don't feel bound by arbitrary rules. This selectivity means your yeses are meaningful and your commitments are genuine.

**Very Low:** You resist imposed obligations and operate from internal motivation rather than external rules. This autonomy is powerful, when you commit to something, it's because you truly choose it. The challenge is navigating systems (work, school) that expect conformity to external obligations.

### Achievement-Striving (C4), Drive to accomplish and achieve

**Very High:** You have an intense drive to achieve and excel. You're likely ambitious, goal-oriented, and constantly pushing yourself. This achievement motivation has probably led to significant accomplishments. The challenge is knowing when to rest and recognizing that your worth isn't defined by what you achieve.

**High:** You're goal-oriented and work hard to accomplish what you set out to do. You take satisfaction in achievement and likely have a track record of success.

**Average:** You have moderate achievement motivation, you work hard on what matters but don't need constant external validation through accomplishments.

**Low:** You define success on your own terms rather than society's. You're not driven by traditional markers of achievement. This independence from external validation is liberating and authentic.

**Very Low:** You have a beautifully non-competitive, process-oriented approach to life. You're more interested in the journey than the destination, in being than in achieving. This is rare and valuable in an achievement-obsessed culture. Your worth is intrinsic, not earned.

### Self-Discipline (C5), Ability to persist at tasks despite difficulty

**Very High:** You have remarkable willpower and persistence. When you commit to a task, you see it through even when it's difficult or boring. This self-discipline is one of the strongest predictors of long-term success. You can do hard things consistently.

**High:** You generally have good follow-through and can persist at tasks even when they're not immediately rewarding. Your self-discipline serves you well.

**Average:** Your discipline varies by task and context, strong for things you care about, harder for things you don't. This is normal and human.

**Low:** You work best with external structure, deadlines, or accountability. Internal motivation alone may not be enough for tedious tasks. This isn't a flaw, many brilliant ND minds work this way. The key is designing your environment to support your natural style.

**Very Low:** You require significant external scaffolding to persist at uninteresting tasks. But here's what others miss: when you're engaged, you have hyperfocus abilities that disciplined people can only dream of. Your challenge isn't willpower, it's finding the right match between task and interest.

### Cautiousness (C6), Tendency to think before acting

**Very High:** You are exceptionally careful and deliberate. You think things through thoroughly before acting, considering multiple angles and potential outcomes. This caution prevents many mistakes and leads to well-considered decisions. The challenge is avoiding analysis paralysis.

**High:** You tend to think before you act and consider consequences. You're not impulsive, which serves you well in important decisions.

**Average:** You balance thought and action, you consider important decisions but don't overthink minor ones. This adaptability serves you well.

**Low:** You prefer action over deliberation. You learn by doing rather than by analyzing. This bias for action means you try more things, fail faster, and learn quicker than cautious people. Many successful entrepreneurs have this pattern.

**Very Low:** You have a powerfully action-oriented approach. While others are still planning, you've already tried three approaches and learned what works. This experimental mindset, combined with resilience from past "failures," makes you incredibly adaptable and fast-learning.

## Extraversion

### Friendliness (E1), Warmth and positive feelings toward others

**Very High:** You have an extraordinarily warm and welcoming presence. People likely feel comfortable and accepted around you. Your friendliness creates connection and builds community wherever you go. This warmth is a genuine gift to the people in your life.

**High:** You're a warm, friendly person who generally makes others feel at ease. You likely have good social rapport and enjoy positive interactions.

**Average:** You're friendly with people you know and can be warm when you choose to be. You're selective about social warmth rather than indiscriminately effusive.

**Low:** You're reserved in your warmth, not cold, but selective about who you open up to. When you do express warmth, it's meaningful because it's earned. Quality over quantity.

**Very Low:** You have a deeply private warmth that you share only with a trusted few. This isn't lack of caring, it's a different distribution. The people you care about know it profoundly because your warmth is concentrated and genuine, not diffuse.

### Gregariousness (E2), Preference for social company

**Very High:** You genuinely love being around people. Social interaction energizes you. You likely have a large social network and feel most alive in groups. Your sociability brings people together and creates community.

**High:** You enjoy social company and generally prefer being with others to being alone. You likely have an active social life.

**Average:** You enjoy socializing in moderation, you need both people-time and alone-time. This balance is healthy.

**Low:** You prefer smaller groups or one-on-one interactions to large social gatherings. You may find extended socializing draining. This selectivity means your social time is meaningful and intentional.

**Very Low:** You're strongly introverted, social interaction requires energy, and solitude restores you. This isn't social anxiety or dislike of people; it's a neurological difference in how you process stimulation. Your depth in one-on-one relationships often exceeds what gregarious people achieve in groups.

### Assertiveness (E3), Tendency to take charge and lead

**Very High:** You're a natural leader who speaks up, takes charge, and influences others. You're comfortable in the spotlight and effective at directing groups. This assertiveness means things happen when you're involved.

**High:** You're generally comfortable speaking up, expressing your opinions, and taking the lead when needed. You can assert yourself effectively.

**Average:** You're assertive in some situations (especially those you care about) and more reserved in others. Context matters.

**Low:** You prefer to influence through collaboration rather than direct assertion. You may lead from behind or through example rather than explicit direction. This collaborative style builds buy-in and shared ownership.

**Very Low:** You have a powerfully non-dominant approach to influence. You lead through service, through expertise, through quiet example. When you do speak up, people listen because it's rare and considered. Many of the most effective leaders in history had this pattern.

### Activity Level (E4), Pace and energy of daily life

**Very High:** You live life at a fast pace with high energy. You're always doing something, always in motion. This vitality means you accomplish a lot and experience a lot. The challenge is remembering to slow down and rest.

**High:** You tend to be active and energetic, preferring to keep busy rather than be idle. You likely pack a lot into your days.

**Average:** You have a moderate activity level, busy when you need to be, restful when you can be. This balance serves you well.

**Low:** You prefer a slower, more deliberate pace of life. You're not lazy, you just don't see the value in constant busyness. Your calmer rhythm can be grounding for others and sustainable for yourself.

**Very Low:** You have a naturally slow, contemplative pace. While the world rushes around you, you move at the speed of thought and intention. This isn't low energy, it's energy directed inward, toward depth rather than breadth. Many deep thinkers have this pattern.

### Excitement-Seeking (E5), Desire for stimulation and thrills

**Very High:** You crave stimulation, excitement, and novelty. Routine bores you. You need intensity to feel alive. This hunger for experience drives you to try bold things and live fully. The challenge is finding healthy outlets for this need.

**High:** You enjoy excitement and stimulation. You probably like trying new things and get restless with too much routine.

**Average:** You enjoy some excitement but also value calm and stability. You can appreciate both a thrilling experience and a quiet evening.

**Low:** You prefer calm, predictable environments. You don't need external thrills to feel engaged, your inner life provides sufficient stimulation. This stability is grounding.

**Very Low:** You have a profoundly low need for external stimulation. You're content with quiet, routine, and predictability. While others chase novelty, you find richness in depth, familiarity, and mastery. This isn't boring, it's the foundation of expertise and deep peace.

### Cheerfulness (E6), Tendency to experience positive emotions

**Very High:** You have a naturally sunny disposition. You experience joy, enthusiasm, and positive emotions readily. Your cheerfulness likely lifts the mood of those around you. This positive affect is contagious and resilience-building.

**High:** You tend to be optimistic and upbeat. You experience positive emotions frequently and can usually see the bright side.

**Average:** Your mood varies normally, you experience both positive and negative emotions appropriately. You're not Pollyanna, but you're not Eeyore either.

**Low:** You have a more serious, contemplative emotional baseline. You don't experience exuberant joy frequently, but when you do, it's meaningful. Your emotional range may skew toward depth rather than height.

**Very Low:** You have a deeply serious, perhaps melancholic baseline. This isn't depression, it's a temperament. Your capacity for profound joy may be less frequent but more intense. Many of history's greatest artists, thinkers, and leaders have had this pattern. Your seriousness is the flip side of your depth.

## Agreeableness

### Trust (A1), Belief in the sincerity and good intentions of others

**Very High:** You have an exceptionally trusting nature. You believe in people's goodness and give them the benefit of the doubt. This trust creates openness and connection. The challenge is protecting yourself from those who might take advantage.

**High:** You're generally trusting and assume good intentions in others. This openness helps you build relationships quickly.

**Average:** You're reasonably trusting but not naive. You evaluate people individually rather than trusting or distrusting indiscriminately.

**Low:** You're cautious about trusting others until they've proven themselves. This skepticism protects you from betrayal and disappointment. Your trust, once earned, is meaningful.

**Very Low:** You have a deeply cautious approach to trust, likely forged by experience. This isn't paranoia, it's wisdom. When you do trust someone, it's because they've truly earned it, and that trust is profound.

### Morality (A2), Frankness and sincerity in dealing with others

**Very High:** You have an exceptionally strong commitment to honesty and authenticity. You cannot be fake, it's viscerally uncomfortable. This integrity is admirable and rare. Others know that when you speak, it's genuine. The challenge is navigating situations that require tact or diplomacy.

**High:** You value honesty and authenticity highly. You prefer to be straightforward in your dealings with others.

**Average:** You're generally honest but can be tactful when needed. You balance authenticity with social grace.

**Low:** You're pragmatic about honesty, you value truth but understand that complete transparency isn't always appropriate. This social intelligence serves you well.

**Very Low:** You have a highly pragmatic, strategic approach to communication. You understand that different contexts call for different levels of disclosure. This isn't dishonesty, it's social sophistication. You're the person who can navigate complex social dynamics that blunt honesty would destroy.

### Altruism (A3), Willingness to help others

**Very High:** You have an extraordinary capacity for giving. Helping others isn't just something you do, it's part of who you are. Your altruism likely extends to strangers as well as loved ones. This generosity of spirit is beautiful. The challenge is ensuring you have enough left for yourself.

**High:** You're a genuinely helpful person who enjoys assisting others. You likely volunteer, offer support to friends, or work in a helping profession.

**Average:** You help when you can and when it's important, but also maintain healthy boundaries. This balanced approach is sustainable.

**Low:** You're selective about your giving, you help those closest to you or causes you deeply care about, but don't spread yourself thin. This focused generosity is meaningful.

**Very Low:** You have a highly discerning approach to helping. You're not indiscriminately generous, you give strategically, where it matters most. This isn't selfishness; it's wisdom about limited resources. The help you do offer is impactful because it's considered.

### Cooperation (A4), Willingness to compromise and avoid conflict

**Very High:** You have an exceptionally cooperative nature. You prioritize harmony, avoid conflict, and are always willing to compromise. This makes you easy to work with and creates peaceful environments. The challenge is ensuring your own needs don't get lost in the pursuit of harmony.

**High:** You tend to be cooperative and prefer to avoid conflict when possible. You're willing to compromise for the greater good.

**Average:** You're cooperative when it makes sense but can stand your ground when something important is at stake. This balance is healthy.

**Low:** You're independent-minded and willing to disagree when you believe you're right. You don't compromise your principles for the sake of harmony. This integrity is valuable.

**Very Low:** You have a powerfully independent, principled stance. You don't compromise on what matters to you, even when it creates friction. This isn't stubbornness, it's having a backbone. Many innovators and reformers have this pattern. The world changes because people like you refuse to go along.

### Modesty (A5), Humility and self-effacement

**Very High:** You have exceptional humility. You downplay your achievements, deflect praise, and see yourself as no better than others. This modesty is genuine and endearing. The challenge is owning your accomplishments and allowing yourself to be celebrated.

**High:** You're a modest person who doesn't seek the spotlight. You're comfortable letting others take credit.

**Average:** You have a balanced view of yourself, you acknowledge your strengths without arrogance and your weaknesses without shame.

**Low:** You're comfortable acknowledging your abilities and accomplishments. You don't hide your competence. This healthy self-regard serves you well.

**Very Low:** You have a strong, confident self-presentation. You know what you're good at and aren't afraid to say so. This isn't arrogance, it's accurate self-assessment combined with comfort in your own skin. In a world that tells ND people to shrink themselves, this confidence is revolutionary.

### Sympathy (A6), Empathy and compassion for others

**Very High:** You have an extraordinarily deep capacity for empathy. You feel others' pain as if it were your own. This compassion is a beautiful gift that makes you an incredible friend, partner, and helper. The challenge is protecting yourself from emotional overwhelm, you can't save everyone, and that's okay.

**High:** You're a compassionate person who cares deeply about others' wellbeing. You likely offer emotional support readily and feel moved by others' struggles.

**Average:** You have a healthy capacity for empathy, you care about others without being overwhelmed by their pain.

**Low:** You tend to be more analytical than emotional in your response to others' difficulties. You may want to fix problems rather than sit with feelings. This problem-solving approach has real value.

**Very Low:** You have a distinctly cognitive, analytical approach to others' struggles. While you may not get swept up in emotional sympathy, you often provide the most practical, effective help. Your strength is action and solutions, not emotional mirroring. Many ND adults have this pattern, it doesn't mean you don't care; you care differently.

## Neuroticism (Emotional Stability)

> Note: All neuroticism facets are framed in terms of emotional richness, sensitivity, and depth. Never frame as "instability" or "dysfunction."

### Anxiety (N1), Tendency to worry and anticipate threat

**Very High:** You have an exceptionally active threat-detection system. Your mind constantly scans for potential problems and prepares for them. While this can be exhausting, it's also a genuine survival strength, you rarely get caught off guard. Many highly successful people have this pattern. The key is channeling this vigilance productively rather than letting it run unchecked.

**High:** You tend to worry and anticipate problems. This foresight helps you prepare for challenges, though it can also create unnecessary stress.

**Average:** You worry about realistic concerns but don't get caught in excessive rumination. Your anxiety is proportionate to actual threats.

**Low:** You're generally calm and unworried. You don't spend mental energy anticipating problems that may never happen. This tranquility is a genuine gift.

**Very Low:** You have an exceptionally calm, unflappable nervous system. While others catastrophize, you stay grounded in the present. This emotional steadiness is rare and valuable. You're the person others turn to when everything feels chaotic.

### Anger (N2), Tendency to experience frustration and irritation

**Very High:** You have a strongly activated justice-sensitivity system. You react intensely to unfairness, incompetence, and boundary violations. This isn't "bad temper", it's a finely tuned sense of what should be. Your anger, when directed constructively, is a powerful force for change and boundary-setting.

**High:** You experience frustration and anger more readily than some. You have strong reactions to injustice and incompetence.

**Average:** You get angry appropriately, not too easily, not with excessive difficulty. Your anger is proportional to provocation.

**Low:** You're slow to anger and quick to forgive. You don't hold grudges or stay upset for long. This emotional resilience is admirable.

**Very Low:** You have an exceptionally long fuse and remarkable emotional resilience. It takes a lot to make you angry, and you recover quickly when you do. This emotional stability makes you a calming presence in conflict.

### Depression (N3), Tendency to experience low mood and sadness

**Very High:** You experience emotions with profound depth, including sadness. Your emotional range extends further into melancholy than most people's. This depth, while painful, is also the source of extraordinary empathy, creativity, and wisdom. Many of humanity's greatest artists and thinkers have had this pattern. It's not a flaw, it's a different emotional architecture.

**High:** You're prone to low moods and may experience sadness more deeply than others. You feel the weight of things.

**Average:** Your mood fluctuates within a normal range. You have good days and bad days like everyone else.

**Low:** You have a naturally upbeat, resilient mood. You don't get bogged down in sadness. This emotional buoyancy carries you through difficult times.

**Very Low:** You have remarkable emotional resilience and a naturally positive baseline. Even in genuinely difficult circumstances, you maintain perspective and hope. This isn't denial, it's a fundamentally optimistic temperament.

### Self-Consciousness (N4), Sensitivity to social evaluation

**Very High:** You're exquisitely aware of how you appear to others. You notice every social cue, every potential judgment. While this can feel like a burden, it's also a form of social intelligence that many lack. You're reading the room at a level most people can't. The challenge is remembering that others are far less observant than you think.

**High:** You're aware of social dynamics and care about how you're perceived. You're attentive to social feedback.

**Average:** You're reasonably aware of social evaluation but don't obsess over it. You can relax in social situations.

**Low:** You're relatively unconcerned with others' opinions of you. You do your thing and let people think what they will. This freedom is liberating.

**Very Low:** You have a remarkably thick skin and genuine independence from social evaluation. You wear what you want, say what you think, and live how you choose. This radical authenticity is increasingly rare and genuinely inspiring.

### Impulsiveness (N5), Tendency to act on immediate urges

**Very High:** You have a powerful connection to your immediate desires and impulses. When you want something, you feel it intensely. This immediacy makes you spontaneous, passionate, and fully alive in the present moment. The challenge is the gap between impulse and action, finding space to choose.

**High:** You tend to act on your feelings and desires in the moment. You're spontaneous and may sometimes regret hasty decisions.

**Average:** You can usually resist temptations when you choose to, though you're not immune to impulse.

**Low:** You're good at delaying gratification and thinking before acting on urges. This self-control serves your long-term goals.

**Very Low:** You have exceptional impulse control and delayed gratification ability. While others chase immediate rewards, you stay focused on long-term goals. This discipline is one of the strongest predictors of life success.

### Vulnerability (N6), Tendency to feel overwhelmed under stress

**Very High:** You feel stress intensely and can become overwhelmed more easily than others. Your nervous system is highly sensitive to pressure. But here's what most people miss: this sensitivity also means you feel joy, beauty, and connection more intensely. Your vulnerability isn't weakness, it's the price of a deeply felt life.

**High:** You tend to feel overwhelmed when under significant stress. You may need more recovery time than others after difficult experiences.

**Average:** You handle moderate stress well but can become overwhelmed by significant pressure. This is normal human functioning.

**Low:** You're resilient under pressure and don't get easily overwhelmed. You handle stress with composure.

**Very Low:** You have remarkable stress resilience. While others crumble under pressure, you rise to the occasion. This emotional toughness serves you in crises and high-stakes situations. You're the steady hand others reach for.
