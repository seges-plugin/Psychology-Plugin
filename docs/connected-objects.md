# Personal records and relationships

Use Noesis to organize the records relevant to your own work and life. Available areas can include relationships, finance, learning and outcomes, as well as personal preferences and other information you explicitly provide.

## Choose a record and an action

Tell your assistant which record you mean and what you want to read or change. Use only the operations available in that connection. If the assistant cannot perform the action, keep a useful draft or open the Noesis website.

Different record types have separate permissions. Life task access does not grant access to financial records, relationship notes, health self-reports or other personal objects. Signing in alone does not enable them.

## Review a change

Where a change requires owner review, open signed-in [Account](https://noesis.seges.ai/en/account/), read the proposed content and approve or reject it yourself. Preparing a change is not saving it. Your assistant cannot approve it on your behalf.

For an existing task, change only the fields you intend to change. Leave unknown dates or times unspecified. If a response is unclear, check that same operation before repeating it.

Relationship reflection is non-explicit and focuses on communication, consent and personal boundaries. Do not request or store explicit sexual details.

## Keep records in context

- A private relationship note describes your perspective; it is not a mutual account connection or another person's agreement.
- A manual financial record is information you entered, not proof of a bank balance or a bank transaction.
- A completed learning exercise records practice, not demonstrated mastery.
- An outcome needs its stated evidence; self-reports and documentary evidence are distinct.
- Culture, religion and health information should come from your explicit statements rather than an inference.

These records do not authorize sending messages, making bank transactions or sharing private information with another product.
