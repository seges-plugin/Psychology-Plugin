# Context and memory

Psychology helps with the question or goal you bring. Start with the relevant material available in the conversation or another source you choose. Your assistant may offer examples, possible patterns and a next step; you can correct or reject any suggestion.

## Choose your sources

An AI host can use only the conversations, memories or excerpts it actually makes available. You can select a small relevant excerpt when something is unavailable. You do not need to upload a full account archive.

A summary, a direct statement and an interpretation are different kinds of evidence. Summaries do not become verified personal facts simply because they mention a recent date. Original event dates, memory dates and the period when a statement applied remain separate. An unknown date can stay unknown.

## Decide what to keep

Reflection can stay in the current conversation. When you ask to retain eligible information, Psychology uses the available destination's permission and review flow. You can continue receiving help while a save waits for review.

- Imported conversation context uses its dedicated import controls.
- Eligible personal profile or trajectory changes require the relevant consent and exact owner review.
- Tasks and other workspace records use their own permissions.

A temporary draft may itself require storage permission. Review the exact content in signed-in Noesis before approving it. Your assistant cannot approve a draft for you. A suggestion, preview or approval screen alone does not mean the record was saved.

If a save response is unclear, check the original operation before starting another one. The assistant should report the confirmed result or explain what remains unresolved.

## Keep control

Installing a package, connecting an account or granting one purpose does not grant every other purpose. You can decline a source or action, manage permissions in [Account](https://noesis.seges.ai/en/account/) and keep working with a conversation-only draft.

Private reflection does not authorize commercial sharing, contacting another person, sending a message or operating a bank account. Those actions require their own available service and explicit authorization.

## Age and suitability

Psychology is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not for children under 13 and is unavailable in the EU, EEA and UK. Follow your AI host's age and parent or legal guardian permission requirements. Use only features permitted by your current account controls.

Individual exercises and instruments may have stricter age or suitability requirements; general access does not make every assessment appropriate for ages 13–17. Relationship reflection is non-explicit and focuses on communication, consent, personal boundaries and respectful relationships.

## Availability

The MCP-only package connects tools without bundled skills or local hooks. The workflows package includes guided workflows; manual runtime packages may offer optional local hooks that your host asks you to trust. Available operations depend on your actual connection.
